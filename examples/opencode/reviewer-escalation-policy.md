# Subagent escalation policy

Use the reviewer subagent selectively, not for every task:

- After two unsuccessful repair attempts on the same failure.
- Before finalizing a complex or security-sensitive modification.
- When implementation behavior remains uncertain after investigation.

When requesting a review, provide the relevant files, current hypothesis, failed attempts, and verifier output. Ask for an independent assessment and concrete next steps. Do not ask the reviewer to edit files.
